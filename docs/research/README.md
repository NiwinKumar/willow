Five desk-research reports behind `docs/DESIGN.md`, produced in parallel on Oct 13 and read in full before the brief was written. Each report tags its claims (observed / secondary / proposal) and ends with its sources. Where a report's recommendation was not adopted, `docs/DESIGN.md` section 3.2 says what changed and why.

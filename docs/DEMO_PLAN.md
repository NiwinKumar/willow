# Demo plan

## Hero test: DHA self-tanner split forearm (start Tue Oct 13)
1. Mark two 4 x 4 cm squares on the inner forearm(s) with tape corners. Left = treated, right = control (nothing, or the plain moisturizer if one is used).
2. Small test spot first; wait ~1 h for any irritation.
3. **Baseline:** 5 repositioned frames per square + 5 face + 5 cheek macro.
4. Apply a measured amount (~0.5 ml) of an over-the-counter DHA self-tanner to the treated square only. Gloves; let dry; don't wash for 8 h.
5. Capture both squares at +1 h, +4 h, +8 h (if awake), +24 h (Wed), +48 h (Thu), +72 h (Fri), +96 h (Sat).
6. Same lamp, same settings, card in frame every time.
7. Also on day 1: inner vs outer forearm (tan line) as an instant positive control.
Safety: forearm only, not face; avoid broken skin; stop on irritation; sunscreen outdoors for 24 h.

Expected: published data (mostly lighter skin, contact spectrophotometer) shows ΔE ~5 at 4 h and ~13 at 24 h. Expect smaller shifts on deeper skin and with a phone; measure, don't assume.

## Judge flow (deployed link)
**Built Oct 13: `/judge`** (submit this URL). A guest account is made automatically and given a 4-product routine (retinoid + BHA at night, no sunscreen). Five stops, each live: (1) run the pipeline on hosted samples (`POST /v1/demo/samples/{id}/session` copies `demo/samples/{id}/` into the judge's session, then the normal analyze; since Oct 20 the first sample is Mei's week-1 face check-in, and a sample run is never the judge's baseline); (2) the proof journey (real seeded journey from `GET /v1/demo/journey` when the demo account has sessions; otherwise the stop says "Not available right now", since the illustrative journey was removed Oct 15); (3) live advisor; (4) live routine check + label scan with a drawn sample label; (5) retailer view. A side panel shows live status of every service (`GET /v1/demo/status`) and how Gemini is constrained. Publish samples and the journey with `api/scripts/seed_demo.py` (no deploy needed). Journey frames and results live under `uploads/demo_willow/` and `results/demo_willow/`, inside the bucket's 30-day lifecycle rule; since Oct 19 every one of them carries a temporary hold, which the lifecycle skips, so they last through judging (into December). `seed_demo.py journey` releases the hold before `--reset` and holds everything again when done; `seed_demo.py hold [--release]` does it by hand (needed around `remeasure.py` or `backfill_previews.py` on the demo account, since a held object cannot be overwritten). Release after judging; the lifecycle then deletes them within a day. Samples live under `demo/` and are not affected. `judge-setup` only touches guest accounts.

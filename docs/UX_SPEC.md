# UX spec (owner: Niwin; Harshini reviews capture screens)

## Principles
1. **Calm and honest.** Show uncertainty plainly ("within noise", "trend only"). Never alarm, never flatter.
2. **Inclusive.** Skin tone is descriptive only. No "bad skin", "flaws", "fix", "fairness", "whitening". Prefer "calmer", "more even", "smoother", "fewer blemishes".
3. **Guided.** Every capture step tells the user exactly what to do and checks it live.
4. **Mobile-first** (iPhone Safari is the main device), good on desktop (judges). Large tap targets, readable at arm's length.
5. **Accessible.** WCAG AA contrast; never color-only meaning (icons + text); labels on all inputs; reduced-motion respected.

## Wording per metric (direction -> friendly text)
| metric | user label | decrease means | increase means | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tone_ita | Skin tone (ITA°) | "change detected" | "change detected" | Never "better/worse". Shown in Science panel + product tests. |
| pigment_spots | Dark spots | "fewer/fainter spots" | "more visible spots" | |
| tone_evenness | Tone evenness | "more even" | "less even" | value is unevenness; lower = more even |
| redness | Redness | "calmer" | "more redness" | caveat for deep skin tones |
| blemishes | Blemishes | "fewer blemishes" | "more blemishes" | indicative |
| pores | Pores | "less visible pores" | "more visible pores" | macro only |
| texture | Texture | "smoother" | "rougher" | macro only |
| lines | Lines | "softer lines" | "more visible lines" | indicative |
| dark_circles | Under-eye | "darker under-eye" (more negative ΔL\*) | "brighter under-eye" | value is under-eye minus cheek ΔL\* |
| shine | Shine | "less shine" | "more shine" | indicative; depends on goal |
| glow | Glow | "less glow" | "more glow" | indicative |
Verdict badge text: `real_change` -> "Real change" + friendly text; `within_noise` -> "Within normal photo variation"; `no_baseline` -> "First reading"; `low_quality` -> "Photo quality too low to compare".

## Screens
1. **Front door and Landing.** `/` is the front door: a returning person (account, or a guest who agreed on this device) goes straight to Home; anyone new starts onboarding (`/welcome`: consent, goals, about you), which makes their guest account at its last step; a "Sign in" pill there takes someone with an account to `/signin`, keeping `?next=`. The landing page is at `/about`: hero line, 15-second explainer, three judge buttons: *Explore a real journey* (seeded demo, read-only), *Try with sample photos*, *Try your own selfie*. Sign in (Google) or continue as guest (anonymous).
2. **Consent.** Plain-language: what we store (photos in a private bucket, deleted after 30 days; results in your account), optional "use my anonymized results to improve recommendations and retailer insights" toggle, delete-my-data, cosmetic disclaimer. Versioned.
3. **Goal picker.** Up to 3 of: clearer skin, even tone & fewer dark spots, calmer skin, smoother texture & pores, softer lines, under-eye, oil control & glow. Sets which groups lead the report.
3b. **Places (Oct 18).** "Where should Willow look?", choose one or more cards: *Cheek close-up* ("Recommended"; pores, texture, tone, spots and redness, up close), *Face photo* (lines, under-eye, shine and glow), *Nose close-up* (pores and blemishes), *Forearms* (for testing a product: one treated arm, one untreated). The last one on can't be turned off. Pre-selected: the cheek, plus the face when a goal is only seen there. One quiet line under them: "Have a camera that shoots RAW (DNG)? You can upload those files in a check-in instead; higher quality photos can give steadier readings." Changeable in You (*Places to track* chips). Saved as `profile.track`.
4. **Check-in (Oct 18).** The Check in sheet shows today's places (from `profile.track`) with camera and baseline per place, "N places · about X min", *Change places* (You) and *One place, or RAW files* (the single-place picker below), and one *Start check-in*. The flow then walks every place: a card ("Place i of N · Back/Front camera", the place, one line, three checks, photo count, *Start camera*, *Skip this place today*), the camera, review, upload; each place's upload starts its measurement in the background and moves on to the next place. End: "All photos are in" with each place as *Measuring* or *Skipped today* and *Done*. Closing part-way asks first and says the places already sent keep measuring. Close-ups use the back camera: a tone (and a short vibration) per photo, an optional voice (on/off next to the flip button) since the screen can't be seen; the lens distance shows under the counter ("Ultra wide · about 7 cm away").
   **One check-in only (Oct 18, later).** Every check-in button opens this sheet; there is no place-specific check-in. Under *Start check-in*: *Upload photos instead* (each place opens the file picker: JPEG, HEIC, or DNG for close-ups and arms; line: "Photos from your library or files, place by place. RAW (DNG) works too; higher quality photos can give steadier readings."). No places saved yet: the sheet first shows the Places cards and *Save places*. A place whose baseline has no normal range is topped up in the walk (card note: "These photos join your baseline from {date}, so it gets its normal range."). Forearms read *Arm with product* / *Arm without product*.
   **Single place (before Oct 18, removed).** Choose site (face, cheek close-up, nose close-up, forearm treated/control). Mode: *Calibrated* (iPhone RAW in Lightroom + color card; upload DNG via "Choose File" -> Files) or *Quick selfie* (browser camera; uncalibrated). Checklist with illustrations: card visible, same light, distance, neutral face, hair back, no makeup. Baseline prompt: "Take 5 photos, moving the phone away and back between each."
5. **Upload + processing.** Per-file progress (signed URL PUT), then stages: "Checking photo quality -> Correcting colors -> Measuring -> Explaining". Typical 10-20 s.
6. **Report.** One page (Oct 18): the day's result at `/progress/:day`; `/result/:id` redirects there. Every place of the day on one page, no place pills; tiles show the change in words, the numbers are in the metric detail. Header: what moved, by direction (Oct 18): "2 improved, 1 moved the other way since your baseline". A change is never shown as just "Changed": ◆ *Improved* (toward what people usually want), ◇ *Moved the other way* (gentle, never "worse"), ◆ *Shifted* for skin tone (no direction is better). Same words on cards, chips, calendar days, Home, Wisp widgets and routine outcomes. Groups (Tone & Pigment, Redness, Clarity, Texture & Pores, Lines, Under-eye, Oil & Glow) ordered by goal. Each metric: value + unit (info icon), confidence chip (Measured / Indicative), verdict badge, mini sparkline. Overlay toggle on the photo. Gemini explanation card ("Why this matters", next steps). Quality issues listed with fix tips. Disclaimer.
7. **Timeline / compare.** One page for every place (Oct 18): *Your skin, over time*, no place or capture-mode switch. Metric chart over time with shaded MDC band (chips across places; a metric read at two places is named with its place); markers for routine changes; before/after slider on aligned images; treated vs control view for the forearm test. Calendar (Oct 18): coloured check-in days are buttons that open the day (pointer, 2px hover lift, 0.96 press, 150 ms; reduced motion dims instead of moving; focus ring); other days stay flat. Under it, one muted line "Tap a coloured day to open it." until a day has been opened from the calendar once on this device.
8. **Routine.** List AM/PM products; add from the catalog (Oct 18: the "Add a product" sheet keeps one height (92dvh) whatever it shows, so it never jumps while searching or filtering; it opens on "Search catalog" with the switcher's pill already on it; the search field, with a search icon, sits beside the switcher; under them "Search the catalog" with the catalog's size, or "N results for “…”" / "N matches" when searching or filtering; concern chips are multi-select (products made for all picked; "Showing products made for all N." and a Clear link past one); products already in the routine are listed last as "✓ In routine"; nothing found offers "Request it", and the list ends with "Not here? Request it"). **Request a product** (second tab, replaces Scan label and Add it yourself): one line on how it works ("We check the product, add it to the catalog with its real ingredients, and let you know when it’s in. The more people ask for a product, the sooner it’s added."), "Photo of the pack" (Gemini reads brand and name into the form, AI-read tag; a catalog match shows "Good news: it’s already in the catalog" with Add to routine) and "Try a sample photo", Brand and Product name, "Send request" (a photo, or both brand and name), then "Requested" with how many others asked, and "Your requests" (Checking / Added with Add / Couldn’t add). On Routine, an answered request shows once as "Added to the catalog" (Add to routine) or "About your request". No focus rings anywhere in the app (Oct 18). Home's "Is your routine working?" card (replaces the old "Next step" card; no eyebrow label) shows how much the routine is moving the skin, product by product, from measured numbers only (`web/src/lib/effect.ts`, built on the Routine outcome rule): a headline ("N of M products show a real change in the direction you want."), a tally (◆ helping · ○ no change yet · ▲ moving the other way · ◌ too early), and up to four product cards (one per row), each with its photo, the metric it targets and the way it moved ("Redness: calmer"), "Day N", and a trend chart to its right (below it on a narrow card) from the last check-in before it started: the person's baseline as a dashed line, their normal range as a soft band, the first reading as a ring and the latest as a dot, and a caption saying how to read it ("Redness · lower means calmer"); the card's footer explains the dashed line and the band, so the chart itself carries no text. The line under the product name says how it is doing (Helping, Moving the other way, Changed, No change beyond your normal range, Too early to tell) and never repeats the caption). A short "△" line with "Ask Wisp" appears for a product that moved the other way ("Worth reviewing"), shows no change beyond the normal range after 8 weeks and 3 check-ins ("You may not need it"), or shares an active with another product (the routine check's duplicates: "You may not need both"). "Direction you want" is lower for spots, evenness, redness, blemishes, pores, texture, lines and shine, higher for under-eye brightness and glow; skin tone has none and is only "changed". Products that target nothing Willow measures (cleansers) are left out; the card is hidden when none can be judged. Footer: "Measured against your own normal range. Timing is an association, not proof." The "Routine today" header shows the check's first finding as quiet text in its right corner. Earlier: Gemini compatibility check: conflicts (severity + how to fix, e.g. "use on alternate nights"), duplicates, gaps (e.g., "no sunscreen while using a retinoid"). Link each product to its outcome on your timeline.
   *Sponsored card (Oct 16; RETAILER_INSIGHTS.md "Proof-gated promotion").* Below the person's own routine, at most one small card: "Sponsored · Willow Proven" first (text, not a tooltip), packshot, name and brand, the evidence line with n and cohort label, "Why am I seeing this?" (the matched goals / skin type + the disclosure), "View product" (the product sheet) and "Hide sponsored" (toast with Undo). Never in the demo journey; never above the routine, the check or Suggested today; never styled like an organic suggestion (no aura, no ember). Retail campaign stats: delivery counts are shown as counted; the holdout comparison appears once each group has 10 people.
   *How it feels (Oct 17; self-reported, `PUT /v1/routine/{id}/feel`).* The person's own verdict on a routine product, kept next to the readings and never mixed into them or shown as a score. Words and ids in `web/src/features/routine/feel.ts` (same as `api/app/feel.py`). **Product sheet** (any routine entry, current or stopped), just above "Your readings since start": "How does it feel?" with three one-tap verdicts ("It's working for me", "Too soon to tell", "Not for me"), then, once answered, up to 4 look-and-feel chips (good ones first). Saves on tap, optimistic; controls disabled while saving; a failure reverts and shows an inline error. Then "You said “…” in week N" ("in your first week" for week 0), one deterministic line setting the answer next to the readings when the product is in use and has an outcome (never a cause; e.g. felt working, readings within range: "Readings often trail what you feel. Keep checking in weekly to see if they catch up."; nothing when the product targets nothing Willow measures), and the privacy line "Only you see your answer. Brands only see totals from 10 or more people who share anonymous insights." **Routine quick check**, below the routine check card, one product at a time: "Quick check: how's {product} after {N} weeks?" ("how was … over N weeks?" when stopped) with the three verdicts. Asks about a product in use 7+ days with no answer in the last 14 days, or stopped in the last 14 days after 7+ days and never answered; oldest start first. A tap shows "Thanks, saved." with "Add how it feels" (opens the product sheet scrolled to the card) and no second product that visit; "Not now" hides it for 7 days on this device. Never in the demo journey. **Routine rows** show the latest answer as a quiet outlined chip "You: …"; a product not answered yet (and already started) shows the same outlined chip, a little fainter and tappable, "How does it feel?", which opens the product sheet scrolled to the card.
   *You → "Sponsored suggestions" switch* (on by default) sits with the privacy receipt; off hides the card everywhere (`profile.hide_sponsored`).
9. **Wisp, the assistant (advisor chat).** Named Wisp in all copy since Oct 18 ("Ask Wisp about this result"; no label tag in the panel header); its face in the chat is the shadow cat (no blue orb): beside each answer (still; speaking while that answer is revealed), thinking beside the steps, listening on the empty chat while the question field has focus. Suggested prompts ("Is my niacinamide doing anything?", "What should I add for dark spots under 1,500?"). Answers cite your measured results and catalog products; product cards with multi-store buttons.
   *Wisp's own button (Oct 18).* Wisp opens from a floating button bottom right (phones: above the dock). Opening, the cat shrinks away and the advisor grows out of its spot; closing, the advisor folds back into the corner and the cat pops out again (reduced motion: a fade), not from the sidebar or the dock. The button is the shadow cat from the avatar pack (`web/src/components/assistant/`), drawn and animated in code: it thinks while a question is out, speaks while the answer is revealed, gives one happy hop when it lands, listens while hovered or while the question field has focus, dozes after 90 s with no input, and is idle otherwise. Accessible name "Wisp", `aria-expanded` while the panel is open; reduced motion stills the body (it still blinks). Every state and size can be checked at `/lab/avatar` (not linked).
10. **Retailer dashboard** (desktop-first). Banner "Synthetic demo cohort + team data". Tabs: Customer needs next, Product outcomes by profile, Brand/category performance, Assortment gaps, Routine conflicts. Embedded Looker Studio + native charts from `/v1/retailer/insights`.
11. **About / science.** How measurement works (card, RAW, MDC), what we don't measure, fairness results, privacy.

## Spot diary (removed Oct 18)
The spot diary (each mark followed across face check-ins) is gone from the app: Home tile, Wisp widget and face map removed. The api's `GET /v1/spots` is kept but unused.

## States to design
Empty (no sessions yet), first reading (no baseline), processing, partial quality failure, total quality failure (retake tips), engine timeout (retry), offline, demo read-only notices, deleted data.
